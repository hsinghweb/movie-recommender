# Sources

## MovieLens Latest Small Dataset

- **Name:** MovieLens Latest Small
- **Provider:** GroupLens Research, University of Minnesota
- **URL:** https://grouplens.org/datasets/movielens/latest/
- **Direct download:** https://files.grouplens.org/datasets/movielens/ml-latest-small.zip
- **License:** Research and education use permitted. See the README in the dataset for full terms.
- **Citation:** F. Maxwell Harper and Joseph A. Koncel-Kedziorski. *The MovieLens Datasets: History and Context.* ACM Transactions on Interactive Intelligent Systems (TiiS), 5(4), Article 19 (December 2015).
- **Contents used:** `ratings.csv` (100,836 ratings by 610 users on 9,742 movies) and `movies.csv` (movie titles and genres).
